Paper 2: organization x zero skipping exploratory pilot, 22 October 2026

Question: does conventional zero skipping change the relative benefit of raster,
bounding-box and merged-region execution, and do traversal and host costs explain it?
This is a development pilot, not held-out publication evidence or a new mechanism.

Reuse unchanged routed W24 images: original packed-BRAM 2/12/6 lanes (A), and
C07 4/12/4 lanes with runtime skipping off/on (B0/B1). 50 MHz, same board, same
integer weights, same requested output and residual-publication endpoint. Keep A:
the C07-disabled arm is known to be slower, so B1-vs-B0 alone is not sufficient.

Fixed development frames: archived G001 frame indices 0 and 2. No selection by
new timing or activation-density results. Fixed mask indices 0,1,2,5,4,10:
empty, dense, clustered-low, scattered-low, clustered-30tiles, checkerboard-30tiles.
The latter pair holds selected area equal; the low-density pair does not.
Within-mask comparisons hold requested pixels identical. 12 frame/mask cases.

First physical step: qualification on C07, all six organization/skipping arms,
one repetition, every selected output archived and compared byte-for-byte with
existing full dense goldens. These timings do not enter the performance pilot.
Offline new RTL qualification covers disjoint regions at top-left/bottom-right,
bbox vs merged, both skip modes, backpressure and the existing five fault cases.
Historical unchanged-source full-raster qualification remains separately pinned.

After qualification passes: primary ABBA runs A/B/B/A, three repetitions each.
Six measured repetitions per frame/mask/arm across two legs. C07's six arms use
six cyclic rotations across the six repetitions; A's three arms use three.
Warm each arm once per launch. Full byte archives for the first global repetition;
per-request output checksum, ownership and terminal status checks on every run.
Keep all failures and losses. Do not restart a failed attempt under the same ID.
Do not call the two ABBA legs independent-day replication.

Primary timing retains the historical stage boundaries. Separate profiling runs
after primary collection subdivide request setup, descriptor setup, accelerator
kick, cache flush, DMA MMIO, wait, validation and publication. Extra timestamp
calls are disabled in primary runs. Profiling is diagnostic, not substituted into
primary results; its instrumentation may perturb the execution. No UART or JTAG
inside timed requests. Input/condition selection remains outside the timer.

Analysis: show paired within-case organization savings for A, B0 and B1; report
absolute milliseconds and relative effects, wins/losses, both ABBA legs, and the
interaction (organization effect with skip on minus that with skip off). Separate
empty/dense controls. Compare B1 against A, not only B0. Do not aggregate requests
into fictitious independent sessions. Profile fractions are not automatic
removable-overhead bounds; stalls and pipeline traversal overlap with arithmetic.

Baseline challenge: the CPU packer already merges halo intervals; do not create
a naive full-image copy baseline. The hardware scanner still visits unused
positions, so any finding is conditional on this implementation unless a more
efficient conventional traversal is addressed. Audit its measured opportunity
before authorizing a jump engine. Do not infer traversal time by subtracting
geometric positions as though each were an additive cycle.

Advance only for a repeatable organization-by-skipping effect or a specific,
substantial baseline issue. A repeated existing result is robustness, not a
novelty rescue. Select any numerical advancement threshold before primary board
timings are opened; current pilot reports effect sizes without an acceptance claim.
Do not start lane/depth/buffer/precision campaigns automatically. Do not modify the
manuscript or public artifact until there is a completed interpretable result.
No commits or pushes. Prepare locally; ask the user for Zybo access when ready.
