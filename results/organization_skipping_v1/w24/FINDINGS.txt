Organization x conventional zero skipping: completed Zybo pilot
22 October 2026 local time

BOTTOM LINE
Region organization still matters on the zero-skipping engine. Its absolute
time saving remains almost unchanged, while its percentage importance increases.
There is no meaningful reversal of the preferred organization. This strengthens
robustness modestly, not novelty through a new mechanism.

DESIGN
Existing routed W24 engines, one Zybo, 50 MHz, identical integer weights and
requested outputs. A: original packed-BRAM reference, 2/12/6 lanes. B: C07,
4/12/4 lanes, skipping off/on within one bitstream. Both use 180 DSPs.
B-on versus A compares complete implementations, not zero skipping in isolation.
Two archived development images; four sparse masks plus empty/dense controls per
image. Three organizations, six repeats per case/arm, A/B/B/A order.
648 primary records, separate 72-request physical qualification and 72-request
diagnostic profile. All completed replays pass. 180 full selected-output archives
verified across qualification and primary runs; remaining requests retain output
checksum, ownership and status checks. One board sitting, not independent days.

PRIMARY RESULTS
Equal-weight means over eight sparse frame/mask cells, each six repetitions.
Endpoint: complete residual publication, not reconstruction/display.

                         Raster ms    Bounding-box ms    Merged ms
Original reference        118.600          108.796          99.531
C07 skipping off          151.110          141.360         132.530
C07 skipping on            97.149           87.423          78.883

Merged versus raster saves 18.580 ms with skipping off, 18.266 ms with skipping
on. Ratios of aggregate means give 12.30% and 18.80%, respectively. Reference
saving: 19.069 ms (16.08%). Near-constant absolute saving is the main finding.
C07-on merged beats original-reference merged by 20.75% (ratio of means).
C07-off is slower than the original reference: it cannot be the only baseline.

The frozen paired mean-per-cell interaction is +7.45 percentage points versus
raster and +3.65 versus bbox. These are means of cell-level percentage effects,
not differences between ratios of aggregate means. First/second sequence legs:
raster +7.42/+7.48 pp; bbox +3.61/+3.69 pp. This is within-sitting consistency.

Scattered masks favor merged in all three configurations. Clustered masks use
one common rectangle: bbox and merged effectively tie. Tiny sign changes are not
policy reversals. The low-density clustered/scattered masks have different areas;
only the 30-tile clustered/checkerboard pair is area-matched. Within-case outputs
are always identical. Empty controls cost ~0.135 ms in every arm. Dense controls
show effectively equal organization times per engine (~309.76 ms reference,
420.05 ms C07-off, 238.88 ms C07-on), not a region-organization gain.

SEPARATE DIAGNOSTIC PROFILE
C07-on merged mean service: 78.876 ms. Wait: 69.380 ms (87.96%); packing: 1.723 ms;
scatter: 4.304 ms; cache flush: 1.664 ms; publication: 1.616 ms. Region setup:
0.095 ms; accelerator kick: 0.016 ms; DMA MMIO: 0.024 ms.
Wait includes accelerator/DMA progress; it is neither removable CPU scheduling
overhead nor pure arithmetic time. The data do not identify a large descriptor-
launch bottleneck. Copy/scatter costs exist, but fractions are not automatically
achievable speedups. Diagnostic service means differ from primary means by less
than 0.03% across six arms; one profiled repetition is not an overhead-equivalence
study. Primary measurements remain separate and unchanged.

The hardware scanner still visits unused positions. This profile does not
separate scanner, arithmetic, DMA and stalls, so does not validate a jump-engine
opportunity. Do not infer additive traversal cycles from geometric counts.

RESEARCH DECISION
Keep this as supporting evidence for the empirical design study: the region
finding survives conventional skipping on these cases. The existing width study
already found nearly fixed absolute organization savings as compute cost changes.
This adds another way to vary that cost but mostly reinforces that explanation.
It does not establish a new scheduler/architecture or an acceptance probability.
Do not automatically start another parallelism/depth/jump-engine campaign. A new
build needs a distinct hypothesis and a cheap opportunity estimate first.
